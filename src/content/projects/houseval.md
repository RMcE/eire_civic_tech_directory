HouseVal is an interactive map that allows users to explore typical residential property prices and related housing information at local level across Ireland.

The platform is primarily built using residential property sales from the Property Price Register dating from 2010. The data is mapped and processed to improve its usefulness, including removing bulk sales, identifying apartments and adjusting new-build prices for VAT. Historical sales are then adjusted to current price levels using the CSO Residential Property Price Index.

Welcome to EireEstate, a platform dedicated to improving transparency in new build development across Ireland. In recent years, the demand for housing has surged, leading to rapid growth in construction and development projects nationwide. However, the process surrounding new builds—ranging from planning applications to construction progress and compliance—can often be complex, opaque, and difficult for local communities to track.

Our goal is to bridge that gap by providing clear, accessible, and up-to-date information for anyone interested in the development of new housing projects.

We believe that transparency is a cornerstone of responsible development. By making vital information available to the public, we aim to foster trust between developers, local authorities, and communities.
